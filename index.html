Shapes, Materials and Regularity

Lecce Encounters In Calculus Of Variations

Register now!

30 Sep 2026 - 02 Oct 2026

SMR:LEICOV is a series of three meetings to bring together young researchers and experts in the field of Calculus of Variations and Partial Differential Equations. We want to give value to the intersections between shape optimization, materials science and regularity theory.

How to reach Lecce

By plane
Brindisi Airport (Aeroporto del Salento - BDS)

This is the closest airport, about 40 km north of Lecce.

Shuttle bus: a direct service runs from Brindisi Airport to the Lecce City Terminal (Piazzale Carmelo Bene).

Taxi / private transfer: available outside arrivals; drive time ≈ 30–35 minutes.

Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport (BRI)

Located about 165 km away. From Bari airport take a local train to Bari Centrale, then a direct train to Lecce (approx. 1h40m).

By train

Lecce Railway Station (Stazione di Lecce) is connected to Italy's main rail network via Trenitalia, including Frecciarossa and Intercity services from Milan, Rome, Bologna, and Bari. The conference venue is approximately a 5-minute walk (≈400 m) from the station.

Local transport

For local urban lines, bus routes, and parking information, plan your trip and purchase digital tickets via the official local public transport company website: SGM Lecce Official Website.

Accommodation & booking advice

Because the Museo Castromediano is close to both the train station and the southwestern gate of the old town, consider these areas for your stay:

  • Historic Center (Centro Storico): Ideal to be within walking distance of Lecce’s Baroque architecture, restaurants, and sights.
  • Station / Viale Gallipoli Area: Best for logistical convenience — just a few minutes' walk from the station and the venue.

Important: September is high season in Salento. Accommodation fills quickly; book hotels or B&Bs well in advance to secure better rates and availability.

Social event

Social Dinner: The conference social dinner will take place on October 1st. It will feature a fixed-price menu showcasing traditional Salento cuisine. Details on menu options and reservations will be provided soon.
